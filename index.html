About Formix International

Your trusted partner for electronic components

Formix International India Pvt. Ltd. is a leading independent distributor, importer and exporter of electronic components and industrial products. Established in Mumbai in 2015, we partner with OEMs, ODMs and EMS companies across industries worldwide.

From cost optimization and obsolescence management to global sourcing, buffer stock and excess inventory, every component meets strict quality benchmarks, including hard-to-find, obsolete and long-lead-time parts.
